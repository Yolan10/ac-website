Commission Structure

Our transparent three-tier system rewards your growth with increasingly higher commission rates. Your tier level is based on the total number of active customers you refer.

Partner Tier Active Customers Commission Rate Example Monthly Earnings*
🥉 Bronze Partner 0 - 10 customers 20% $1,000 - $2,000
🥈 Silver Partner 11 - 25 customers 25% $2,500 - $6,250
🥇 Gold Partner 26+ customers 30% $7,500+

* Based on average customer plan value of $250/month. Actual earnings will vary based on customer plan selections.

How Commissions Are Calculated

Your monthly commission is calculated using this simple formula:

Monthly Commission = (Total Customer MRR) × (Your Tier Rate)

Example Scenario:

  • You refer 20 customers (Silver Tier - 25% commission rate)
  • Average customer subscription: $250/month
  • Total Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): 20 × $250 = $5,000
  • Your Monthly Commission: $5,000 × 25% = $1,250
  • Your Annual Revenue: $1,250 × 12 = $15,000

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cost to join the partner program?

No, the ActiveCampaign Agency Partner Program is completely free to join. There are no setup fees, monthly fees, or minimum commitments.

How long do I continue to earn commissions?

You earn recurring commissions for as long as your referred customers maintain active subscriptions. If a customer cancels, commissions for that customer stop, but you continue earning from all other active referrals.

Can I refer customers in any country?

Yes, ActiveCampaign serves customers globally. You can refer businesses from any country where ActiveCampaign operates.

What happens if I move to a higher tier?

When you qualify for a higher tier, the new commission rate applies to ALL your active customers (both existing and new), retroactive to the beginning of the month you achieve the new tier.

How do I track my referrals and commissions?

The partner portal provides real-time tracking of all referrals, customer status, commission calculations, and payment history. You'll also receive monthly statements via email.
