Focus: Maximizing revenue and building long-term partnership value

How Commissions Are Calculated

The Basic Formula

Monthly Commission = (Total Customer MRR) × (Your Tier Rate)

Where:

  • Total Customer MRR = Sum of all monthly subscription values from your referred customers
  • Your Tier Rate = 20%, 25%, or 30% based on your active customer count

Detailed Calculation Example - Bronze Tier

Scenario: You have 8 active customers (Bronze Tier - 20%)
Customer 1: $199/month
Customer 2: $299/month
Customer 3: $149/month
Customer 4: $349/month
Customer 5: $199/month
Customer 6: $249/month
Customer 7: $399/month
Customer 8: $179/month
Total MRR: $2,022/month
Your Commission: $2,022 × 20% = $404.40/month
Annual Revenue: $404.40 × 12 = $4,852.80/year

Payment Schedule & Terms

When You Get Paid

  • Monthly Payments: Commissions are calculated at the end of each calendar month
  • Payment Date: Processed within 15 days after month end (typically by the 15th)
  • First Payment: Your first commission payment occurs in the month following your first referral

Payment Methods

  • Direct Deposit (ACH): Available for US bank accounts (fastest option)
  • PayPal: Available globally (small processing fee may apply)
  • Wire Transfer: Available for international partners (fees may apply)

Minimum Payout Threshold: $50 - If your monthly commission is below $50, it will roll over to the following month until the threshold is met.

Tax Documentation

  • US Partners: Must complete W-9 form before first payment
  • International Partners: Must complete W-8BEN form
  • Annual Reporting: 1099 forms issued to US partners earning $600+ annually

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when a customer upgrades their plan?

Great news! Your commission automatically increases because it's based on the customer's current subscription value. If a customer upgrades from $149/month to $299/month, you immediately start earning commission on the higher amount.

What if a customer downgrades?

Your commission adjusts to reflect the new lower subscription value. However, as long as the customer remains active, you continue earning commission.

Do I earn commission during free trials?

No, commissions begin when a customer converts to a paid subscription after their trial period. Once they become a paying customer, you earn commission on every payment they make.

What happens if a customer cancels?

Commission stops for that specific customer when they cancel. However, you continue earning from all your other active customers. Your tier level is based on your current active customer count.

If I drop below a tier threshold, do I lose my tier rate?

Yes, tier rates are based on your current active customer count. If you have 26 customers (Gold - 30%) and some cancel, dropping you to 24 customers, you'll move back to Silver tier (25%) starting the following month. This encourages partners to continuously acquire new customers.

How do tier changes affect existing customers?

Your current tier rate applies to ALL your active customers, regardless of when they signed up. If you upgrade from Silver to Gold, you immediately start earning 30% on all existing and new customers.

Can I see my commission calculations in real-time?

Yes! The partner portal shows live data including:

  • Current active customer count
  • Total Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
  • Current tier and commission rate
  • Month-to-date commission earnings
  • Individual customer subscription values
  • Payment history and upcoming payouts

Are there any deductions from my commission?

No hidden deductions. The commission you see calculated is what you receive (minus any payment processing fees if using certain methods like international wire transfer). For example, PayPal may charge a small receiving fee depending on your country.

What if a customer gets a refund?

If a customer receives a refund for a month they already paid, the corresponding commission for that month will be deducted from your next payment.

How quickly do new referrals count toward my customer total?

As soon as a referred customer completes their first paid month, they count toward your active customer total and tier calculation. Trial users don't count until they convert to paid.

Maximizing Your Commission Earnings

Strategy 1: Focus on Higher-Value Plans

Recommend plans that best fit your client's needs. Larger businesses often need higher-tier plans, which increases your MRR without adding more customers to manage.

Strategy 2: Aim for Tier Upgrades

Each tier upgrade gives you a significant raise on ALL customers. Calculate how many more customers you need to reach the next tier and create a plan to get there.

Strategy 3: Reduce Churn

Help your customers succeed with ActiveCampaign. Provide onboarding support, training, and best practices to keep them active long-term. Lower churn = more stable recurring revenue for you.

Strategy 4: Encourage Upgrades

As your customers grow, they may need more features. Stay in touch and recommend plan upgrades when appropriate—your commission grows along with their business.
