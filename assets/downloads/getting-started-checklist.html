Partner Getting Started Checklist

Your 30-Day Roadmap to Success

Pre-Application Checklist

Before applying to become a partner, make sure you have these essentials ready:

  • Business information (legal name, address, website)
  • Valid business email address (no personal Gmail/Yahoo accounts)
  • Understanding of your target market and ideal customers
  • Basic knowledge of marketing automation concepts
  • Tax ID or Social Security Number (for payment setup)
  • Bank account or PayPal information for commission payments
  • Time commitment: 5-10 hours per week minimum
  • At least 2-3 potential customers ready to refer

💡 Pro Tip: Having a portfolio website or LinkedIn profile showcasing your marketing services significantly improves your application approval chances.

Application Process (Days 1-3)

📝 Phase 1: Submit Application
Timeline: Day 1 (15 minutes)
  • Visit the partner application page
  • Complete the online application form accurately
  • Provide details about your business and experience
  • Describe your target market and customer types
  • Share your marketing and outreach strategy
  • Submit the application for review

💡 What We're Looking For: Agencies and professionals actively serving clients who could benefit from marketing automation. Be specific about your niche and customer base.

⏰ Phase 2: Application Review
Timeline: Days 2-3
  • Wait for email confirmation (typically within 2-3 business days)
  • Check your spam folder for responses
  • Review the partner agreement sent via email
  • Read all terms and conditions carefully
  • Sign the agreement electronically
  • Wait for portal access credentials

What happens next: Once approved, you'll receive a welcome email with:

  • Partner portal login credentials
  • Your unique referral link
  • Access to training resources
  • Partner success manager contact information

Onboarding & Setup (Days 4-7)

🚀 Phase 3: Initial Setup
Timeline: Day 4 (2 hours)
  • Log in to the partner portal for the first time
  • Complete your partner profile (photo, bio, company info)
  • Set up payment preferences (bank or PayPal)
  • Submit tax documentation (W-9 or W-8BEN)
  • Save your unique referral link and tracking code
  • Explore the dashboard and familiarize yourself with features
  • Set up email notifications for new referrals
📚 Phase 4: Essential Training
Timeline: Days 5-7 (4-6 hours total)
  • Complete "Partner Orientation" course (30 minutes)
  • Watch "ActiveCampaign Product Overview" video (45 minutes)
  • Review "Commission Structure Deep Dive" guide (30 minutes)
  • Study "Effective Sales Techniques" module (1 hour)
  • Learn "How to Demo ActiveCampaign" (1 hour)
  • Explore customer success stories and case studies (1 hour)
  • Take the optional partner certification quiz
📖 Recommended Resources:
  • Partner Playbook (comprehensive guide)
  • ROI Calculator template
  • Competitive comparison sheets
  • Email templates for client outreach

First Referrals (Days 8-14)

🎯 Phase 5: Launch Your Outreach
Timeline: Week 2
  • Identify 10-20 potential customers from your network
  • Prepare your elevator pitch (30-second explanation)
  • Download and customize marketing materials
  • Schedule 5-10 intro calls or meetings
  • Share your referral link via email campaigns
  • Post about the partnership on social media
  • Add ActiveCampaign to your website services page
  • Create a simple landing page for referrals (optional)

💡 First Referral Strategy: Start with your best-fit clients first—businesses that would clearly benefit from automation and trust your recommendations.

Growth & Optimization (Days 15-30)

📈 Phase 6: Scale Your Success
Timeline: Weeks 3-4
  • Track your first referral conversions in the portal
  • Schedule your first check-in call with partner success manager
  • Ask for feedback on your pitch and materials
  • Join the partner community forum or Slack channel
  • Attend the monthly partner webinar (if available)
  • Refine your outreach based on what's working
  • Create a goal: reach [X] customers by [date]
  • Document your process for consistent referrals
  • Set up client onboarding workflow for new referrals
  • Plan your path to the next commission tier

Beyond Day 30: Long-Term Success

Monthly Best Practices

  • Review your commission dashboard at month start
  • Check in with existing referred customers
  • Identify 10 new prospects each month
  • Attend partner training sessions and webinars
  • Share success stories (with permission)
  • Update your marketing materials with latest features
  • Track progress toward next tier level
  • Calculate projected annual earnings

Quarterly Goals

  • Aim to add 5-10 new customers per quarter
  • Reduce customer churn (aim for <5% quarterly)
  • Complete advanced training or certification
  • Request case study participation from top customers
  • Review and optimize your referral process
  • Explore co-marketing opportunities with ActiveCampaign
