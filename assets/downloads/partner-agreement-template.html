1. Definitions

  1. "Partner Program" refers to the ActiveCampaign Agency Partner Program as described in the Partner Program Overview document.
  2. "Referred Customer" means any business or individual that signs up for a paid ActiveCampaign subscription through the Partner's unique referral link or tracking code.
  3. "Commission" means the recurring percentage-based payment earned by Partner for each Referred Customer's active subscription.
  4. "Tier Level" refers to the commission rate tier (Bronze, Silver, or Gold) based on the Partner's total number of active Referred Customers.
  5. "MRR" means Monthly Recurring Revenue, calculated as the total monthly subscription fees paid by all of Partner's active Referred Customers.
  6. "Partner Portal" refers to the online dashboard provided to Partner for tracking referrals, commissions, and program resources.

2. Scope of Partnership

  1. Non-Exclusive Partnership: This Agreement establishes a non-exclusive partnership. Both parties may enter into similar arrangements with other entities.
  2. Partner Responsibilities: Partner agrees to promote ActiveCampaign's services to potential customers in a professional manner, providing accurate information about features, pricing, and capabilities.
  3. Referral Process: Partner will use the unique referral link or tracking code provided by Company to ensure proper attribution of Referred Customers.
  4. Territory: Partner may refer customers from any geographic location where ActiveCampaign operates, subject to applicable laws and regulations.

3. Commission Structure

  1. Commission Rates: Partner shall earn recurring commissions based on the following tier structure:
    • Bronze Partner (0-10 active customers): 20% of MRR
    • Silver Partner (11-25 active customers): 25% of MRR
    • Gold Partner (26+ active customers): 30% of MRR
  2. Commission Calculation: Monthly commissions are calculated as:
    Monthly Commission = (Total MRR of all active Referred Customers) × (Current Tier Rate)
  3. Tier Application: The Partner's current tier rate applies to ALL active Referred Customers, regardless of when they were referred. Tier changes take effect at the beginning of the month in which the new tier is achieved.
  4. Commission Duration: Partner earns commissions for as long as a Referred Customer maintains an active paid subscription. Commissions cease when a customer cancels or suspends their account.
  5. Trial Period: Commissions begin only when a Referred Customer converts to a paid subscription after their free trial period.
  6. Upgrades and Downgrades: If a Referred Customer changes their subscription plan, commissions adjust automatically to reflect the new monthly subscription value.

4. Payment Terms

  1. Payment Schedule: Commissions are calculated at the end of each calendar month and paid within 15 days of month end.
  2. Minimum Payout: A minimum commission balance of $50.00 USD is required for payment processing. Balances below this threshold will roll over to the following month.
  3. Payment Methods: Payments will be made via Partner's selected method:
    • Direct deposit (ACH) for US bank accounts
    • PayPal transfer
    • Wire transfer (international, fees may apply)
  4. Currency: All commission payments are made in United States Dollars (USD).
  5. Tax Responsibility: Partner is solely responsible for all taxes associated with commission payments. Partner agrees to provide required tax documentation (W-9 or W-8BEN) before receiving first payment.
  6. Refunds and Chargebacks: If a Referred Customer receives a refund for any payment period, the corresponding commission for that period will be deducted from Partner's next payment.

5. Partner Obligations

  1. Professional Conduct: Partner shall represent Company professionally and accurately, complying with all applicable laws and regulations.
  2. Accurate Information: Partner warrants that all information provided in the application and partner profile is accurate and will be kept up to date.
  3. Brand Guidelines: Partner agrees to follow Company's brand guidelines when using Company name, logo, or marketing materials.
  4. Prohibited Activities: Partner shall NOT:
    • Make false or misleading claims about Company products or services
    • Engage in spamming, unsolicited bulk email, or deceptive marketing practices
    • Refer fraudulent or fake accounts
    • Self-refer or refer related entities without disclosure
    • Violate any applicable laws, including data protection and privacy regulations
    • Disparage Company or its products publicly
  5. Compliance: Partner agrees to comply with all applicable anti-spam laws (including CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL) when promoting Company services.
  6. Customer Support: While Company provides product support to all customers, Partner is encouraged to provide initial guidance and onboarding assistance to Referred Customers.

6. Company Obligations

  1. Portal Access: Company shall provide Partner with access to the Partner Portal for tracking referrals, commissions, and accessing resources.
  2. Timely Payments: Company agrees to process commission payments according to the schedule outlined in Section 4.
  3. Marketing Resources: Company will provide Partner with marketing materials, training resources, and product information.
  4. Partner Support: Company will provide reasonable support to Partner through designated partner success managers and support channels.
  5. Tracking and Attribution: Company will maintain accurate tracking systems to properly attribute Referred Customers to Partner.

7. Intellectual Property

  1. Company IP: All intellectual property rights in Company name, logo, trademarks, products, and services remain the exclusive property of Company.
  2. Limited License: Company grants Partner a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Company trademarks and marketing materials solely for the purpose of promoting Company services under this Agreement.
  3. Partner IP: Partner retains all rights to Partner's own intellectual property, including business name, logo, and proprietary materials.
  4. No Implied Rights: Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any rights not explicitly stated herein.

8. Confidentiality

  1. Confidential Information: Both parties may have access to confidential information including business strategies, customer data, pricing, and proprietary processes.
  2. Non-Disclosure: Each party agrees not to disclose the other party's confidential information to third parties without prior written consent.
  3. Exceptions: Confidential information does not include information that:
    • Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
    • Was rightfully known before disclosure
    • Is independently developed without use of confidential information
    • Must be disclosed by law or court order
  4. Survival: Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

9. Term and Termination

  1. Initial Term: This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.
  2. Termination by Partner: Partner may terminate this Agreement at any time with 30 days written notice to Company.
  3. Termination by Company: Company may terminate this Agreement:
    • With 30 days written notice for any reason
    • Immediately for breach of Agreement terms
    • Immediately if Partner engages in fraudulent or illegal activities
    • Immediately if Partner violates brand guidelines or prohibited activities
  4. Effect of Termination: Upon termination:
    • Partner's access to Partner Portal will be revoked
    • Partner must cease using Company trademarks and marketing materials
    • Partner will receive commission payments for existing Referred Customers for 90 days following termination date (wind-down period)
    • After the 90-day wind-down period, Partner forfeits all future commissions
    • All outstanding payments owed will be settled within 60 days
  5. Survival: Sections 7 (Intellectual Property), 8 (Confidentiality), 10 (Limitation of Liability), and 12 (General Provisions) survive termination.

10. Limitation of Liability

  1. Independent Contractor: Partner is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or legal representative of Company. Partner has no authority to bind Company to any obligation.
  2. No Warranties: EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED IN THIS AGREEMENT, COMPANY MAKES NO WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
  3. Limitation: IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.
  4. Cap on Liability: Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions paid to Partner in the 12 months preceding the claim.
  5. Indemnification: Partner agrees to indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claims arising from Partner's breach of this Agreement or violation of applicable laws.

11. Data Protection and Privacy

  1. Customer Data: Partner may have access to customer contact information through the referral process. Partner agrees to handle all data in compliance with applicable privacy laws.
  2. GDPR Compliance: If Partner refers customers from the European Union, Partner agrees to comply with GDPR requirements.
  3. Data Use: Partner shall use customer data solely for the purpose of facilitating referrals and providing customer support. Partner shall not sell, trade, or share customer data with third parties.
  4. Security: Partner agrees to implement reasonable security measures to protect any customer data in Partner's possession.

  1. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with the Partner Program Overview and related program documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
  2. Amendments: Company may modify this Agreement or program terms with 30 days notice to Partner. Continued participation after notice period constitutes acceptance of changes.
  3. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [State/Country], without regard to conflict of law principles.
  4. Dispute Resolution: Any disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiation. If unresolved, disputes shall be settled through binding arbitration.
  5. Assignment: Partner may not assign this Agreement without prior written consent from Company. Company may assign this Agreement to any successor or affiliate.
  6. Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.
  7. Waiver: Failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver of that provision or any other provision.
  8. Force Majeure: Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform due to circumstances beyond reasonable control.
  9. Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered via email to the addresses provided in this Agreement.
