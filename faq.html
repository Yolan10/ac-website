Getting Started

The Saturnea is designed for marketing agencies, consultants, freelancers, and service providers who want to earn recurring revenue by referring clients to ActiveCampaign. Partners receive commissions on every customer they refer, along with access to training, resources, and dedicated support.

The program is open to marketing agencies, consultants, freelancers, web developers, business coaches, and anyone who works with businesses that could benefit from marketing automation. We look for partners who are committed to customer success and have experience helping businesses grow.

Simply fill out our online application form with information about your business, experience, and how you plan to promote ActiveCampaign. The application takes 5-10 minutes to complete. Our team will review your application and respond within 2-3 business days.

No! The ActiveCampaign Partner Program is completely free to join. There are no signup fees, monthly fees, or hidden costs. You only earn money—we never charge you.

You'll need an active business (website or portfolio), professional email address, experience in marketing or related fields, and a commitment to helping customers succeed. During the application, we'll ask about your business, client base, and how you plan to promote ActiveCampaign.

Commissions & Earnings

Partners earn 20-30% recurring monthly commission based on their tier level. Bronze Partners (0-10 customers) earn 20%, Silver Partners (11-25 customers) earn 25%, and Gold Partners (26+ customers) earn 30%. Commissions are paid monthly as long as your referred customers remain active.

Recurring commission means you continue earning every month that your referred customer maintains their ActiveCampaign subscription. Unlike one-time referral fees, you earn passive income month after month, year after year, creating a sustainable revenue stream.

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid out within 30 days after the end of each month. For example, commissions earned in January are paid by the end of February. You can track your earnings in real-time through your partner portal.

Commissions are based on the monthly subscription fee paid by customers you refer. For example, if you're a Silver Partner (25% commission) and refer a customer paying $150/month, you earn $37.50 per month for as long as that customer remains active.

We offer multiple payment options including direct deposit (ACH), PayPal, and wire transfer. You can select your preferred payment method in your partner portal settings.

Yes, the minimum payout threshold is $50. If your monthly commissions are below $50, they'll roll over to the following month until the threshold is met.
