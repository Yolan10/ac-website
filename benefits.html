Partner Program Benefits

Unlock recurring revenue, exclusive resources, and growth opportunities that scale with your business.

Everything You Need to Succeed

We provide comprehensive support and resources to help you grow your business and deliver exceptional value to your clients.

💰

Recurring Revenue Commissions

Earn up to 30% recurring monthly commission on every customer you refer. Unlike one-time referral fees, you continue earning as long as your referred customers remain active—creating predictable, passive income that compounds over time.

📈

Tiered Commission Structure

The more customers you refer, the higher your commission rate. Start at 20% and unlock up to 30% recurring commission as you grow your referral base. Plus, earn bonus commissions for hitting monthly and quarterly targets.

🎓

Comprehensive Training & Certification

Access our complete training library including video courses, webinars, and hands-on workshops. Become a certified ActiveCampaign expert and earn credentials that demonstrate your expertise to potential clients.

🛠️

Professional Marketing Resources

Get access to co-branded sales collateral, presentation templates, case studies, email templates, and social media assets. Everything you need to promote ActiveCampaign professionally to your clients.

🤝

Dedicated Partner Support

Priority access to our partner support team who understand the unique needs of agencies and consultants. Get help with sales questions, technical issues, and strategic advice to close more deals.

📊

Partner Portal & Dashboard

Track your referrals, monitor commission earnings, and access performance reports through your dedicated partner portal. Real-time visibility into your partner business with detailed analytics and insights.

🎯

Co-Marketing Opportunities

Qualify for featured partner listings, co-branded webinars, case study opportunities, and joint marketing campaigns that help you reach new customers and build your brand.

🌐

Partner Community Access

Connect with thousands of successful partners worldwide. Share best practices, learn from case studies, and network with other agency owners and consultants in our exclusive partner community.

🔧

Technical Support & Resources

Access detailed API documentation, integration guides, developer resources, and technical support to help you build custom solutions for your clients.

⚡

Early Access to New Features

Be the first to know about and test new ActiveCampaign features. Provide feedback that shapes product development and stay ahead of the curve with cutting-edge capabilities.
