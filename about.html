About the Agency Partner Program

Empowering agencies, consultants, and freelancers to deliver exceptional customer experiences while building sustainable, recurring revenue streams.

Our Mission

To empower our partners with the tools, knowledge, and support they need to help businesses succeed with ActiveCampaign.

Partnering for Mutual Success

The ActiveCampaign Agency Partner Program is designed for marketing agencies, consultants, and freelancers who want to grow their business while helping their clients succeed with marketing automation.

We believe that your success is our success. That's why we've built a program that not only rewards you with competitive recurring commissions but also provides you with comprehensive training, dedicated support, and the resources you need to become an ActiveCampaign expert.

  • Access to world-class marketing automation platform
  • Comprehensive training and certification programs
  • Dedicated partner support team
  • Co-marketing opportunities and resources
  • Recurring revenue opportunities
