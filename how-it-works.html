How the Partner Program Works

Four simple steps to start earning recurring revenue with ActiveCampaign.

1

Apply to Join

Complete our simple application form with information about your business, experience, and why you want to become a partner.

2

Get Approved

Our partner team reviews your application within 2-3 business days and contacts you with next steps.

3

Onboarding & Training

Access your partner portal, complete initial training, and get your unique referral link and marketing materials.

4

Start Earning

Refer customers, track your commissions, and receive monthly payouts while growing your business.

Detailed Process

Step 1: Submit Your Application

Timeline: 5-10 minutes

Fill out our partner application form with details about your business, services, client base, and experience with marketing automation. We want to understand how you'll help businesses succeed with ActiveCampaign.

  • Business information and website
  • Your experience and expertise
  • Target market and client types
  • How you plan to promote ActiveCampaign

Step 2: Application Review

Timeline: 2-3 business days

Our partner team reviews your application to ensure you're a good fit for the program. We look for professionals who are committed to customer success and have experience helping businesses grow.

  • Review of business credentials
  • Assessment of experience and expertise
  • Verification of website and online presence
  • Approval decision and notification

Step 3: Onboarding & Setup

Timeline: 1-2 weeks (self-paced)

Once approved, you'll receive access to your partner portal and onboarding materials. Complete the initial training to get up to speed on ActiveCampaign features, partner program details, and best practices.

  • Partner portal access and setup
  • Platform training and certification (optional)
  • Receive unique referral links and tracking codes
  • Access marketing resources and sales tools
  • Schedule kickoff call with partner manager
🚀

Step 4: Start Referring Customers

Timeline: Ongoing

Begin promoting ActiveCampaign to your clients and prospects using your unique referral link. When customers sign up through your link, they're automatically tracked to your account.

  • Share your referral link with prospects
  • Use co-branded marketing materials
  • Offer ActiveCampaign as part of your services
  • Track referrals in your partner dashboard

Step 5: Earn Recurring Commissions

Timeline: Monthly payouts

Receive monthly recurring commissions for every active customer you refer. Commissions are calculated based on customer subscription fees and your current partner tier.

  • Automatic commission tracking
  • Monthly payout via your preferred method
  • Detailed commission reports
  • Lifetime recurring revenue on referrals

Step 6: Grow & Scale

Timeline: Ongoing

As you refer more customers, unlock higher commission tiers, bonus opportunities, and exclusive partnership benefits. Scale your revenue while helping more businesses succeed.

  • Progress to higher commission tiers
  • Unlock co-marketing opportunities
  • Get featured in partner directory
  • Access advanced resources and support
